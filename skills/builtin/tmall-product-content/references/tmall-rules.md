# Tmall rules for product content

These are the rules the Tmall product-content checks read. Every rule has a value, a status and a source (an address and the date it was read or checked). The status says how far the rule can be trusted:

- `confirmed`: read on a current official Tmall, Tmall Global, regulator or legal page; the page address and the date read are in the source.
- `from-spec`: fixed by the approved design (`work/docs/specs/2026-10-25-tmall-product-content-spec.md`, in the private work folder) or by one of Easel's own rule files; the source names the file and the date it was checked.
- `proposal`: written by us; no page read states it. The source says "not confirmed because" and why.
- `unconfirmed`: Tmall guidance was searched for and no current official page states it; the value is the most careful lead and nothing more. The source says "not confirmed because" and why.

Anything that is not `confirmed` or `from-spec` is listed in every stage 6 check report under "not confirmed against current Tmall guidance". Nothing here is silently trusted.

### Order of precedence (the owner's rule, issue 09)

This file is the reading of the platform's published standards. It comes first because the work sessions have to start from what the platform says, but the owner ruled it is a fallback only: the platform's size guides differ, so the standards are read and extracted before any work or rule is decided, and a competitor's case always wins over this reading for what sells (their numbers do not lie), including any design or wording that contradicts a rule here. It came from the recheck of the Tmall plan choices (choice 31, whether a main image must be exactly 800 by 800).

In practice: read this file first; then let the competitor cases (stage 2 notes) decide, in stage 3, what each product's pages do. Where a studied competitor case shows what sells and its design or wording contradicts a rule below, the case wins. This file only fills the gaps the cases leave. Nothing in the code lets a case override a stage 6 check yet; that is booked separately, after the competitor-reading trial (issue 02). Whether "the case always wins" also covers wording the advertising law restricts is the owner's question and is not decided here (brief, Outstanding decisions).

## Where the pages disagree

Recorded, not decided.

- First and second main image text: the Tmall Global general page (in force 2026-04-13) says the first and second main images carry no promotional or exaggerated text (seckill, limited-time discount, free shipping, "N% off", "spend N", "free N"). The older Tmall Global health-product page (2019) lets the second image show selling points and promotions in fixed positions. The mainland food pages say the white-background images carry no exaggerated promotional or descriptive text. Our headline main image carries text, so the stage 3 plan has to place the text-free images first or take this up with the team; a competitor case that shows otherwise wins.
- Logo size: the Tmall Global general page says a tenth of the image; the mainland food and health pages say up to four tenths of the width and two tenths of the height.
