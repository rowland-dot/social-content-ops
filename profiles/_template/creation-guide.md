# Creation guide: <account name>

Every agent that draws images or writes posts for this account reads this whole file first. Each rule says which kind of card or post it applies to; a rule with no stated scope does not belong here. Rules that hold for every account live in the skills, not here.

Example below is for a made-up account, "Acme Test", a home-baking account. Replace it.

## Images

What this account's pictures show, and what they never do.

- Kind of card it applies to: cover. Rule: the photo leads with the finished loaf on a wooden board, daylight from the left. Why: the account's covers are judged on the first second of the feed.
- Kind of card: step cards (how to). Rule: one hand in frame, at most three props. Why: steps stay readable at phone size.
- Setting: a lived-in home kitchen; no studio backdrops.
- This account never shows: a face in the cover photo.

## Posts

Voice, claims and formats.

- Kind of post: recipe posts. Voice: a friendly neighbour, short sentences, one tip per card.
- Kind of post: any. Claims: no health claims about bread; say what the reader will see and do.
- Format: title up to 20 characters; five to eight hashtags, the first is the account's own tag.
